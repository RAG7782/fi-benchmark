### 1.2 FOUNDATIONAL CONCEPTS — HIGH SEMIOTIC DENSITY GLOSSARY

Each term below activates a specific professional frame. Internalize each as a **decision node**, not merely a definition.

**CONSUMPTION TAX ARCHITECTURE**
- **IBS (Imposto sobre Bens e Serviços)**: Subnational VAT (states + municipalities), dual-rate structure, administered by Comitê Gestor. Destination-principle. Non-cumulative absolute. Replaces ICMS + ISS over 2026–2032 split-rate transition.
- **CBS (Contribuição sobre Bens e Serviços)**: Federal VAT-equivalent, replaces PIS/COFINS. Non-cumulative, full credit. Administered by RFB. Shares IBS base but separate legal nature (contribuição vs. imposto).
- **IS (Imposto Seletivo)**: "Sin tax" / extrafiscal excise on goods harmful to health and environment. Applies to tobacco, alcohol, vehicles, weapons, financial assets (contested). NOT creditable against IBS/CBS. Creates cascading effect for certain supply chains.
- **Alíquota de Referência**: National benchmark rate for IBS (≈27.9% combined estimate — subject to Senate resolution). Deviation by states/municipalities permitted within constitutional bands.
- **Cesta Básica Nacional**: Zero-rate basket — critical classification decision for food industry clients.
- **Regime de Transição 2026–2032**: Split coexistence period. 2026: CBS 0.9% + IBS 0.1% (test rates). 2027: PIS/COFINS extinguished, CBS full. 2029–2032: progressive ICMS/ISS phase-out. **This creates a mandatory dual-compliance burden for 6 years.**
- **Não-cumulatividade Plena**: Full input credit regime under IBS/CBS — broader than current PIS/COFINS non-cumulative regime. Includes credits on fixed assets (imobilizado), personnel costs partially, financial services (contested).
- **Split Payment**: Automatic tax withholding at payment gateway level — operational impact on cash flow management.

**INCOME TAX ARCHITECTURE (IN FLUX)**
- **IRPJ/CSLL**: Corporate income tax. Base rate 15% + adicional 10% over R$240k/year IRPJ + 9% CSLL = effective ~34%. Under reform pressure (Projeto de Reforma do IR — PL 1.087/2024).
- **JCP (Juros sobre Capital Próprio)**: Notional interest deduction on equity. **Lei 14.789/2023 severely restricted** JCP — no longer deductible against bases accrued from 2024 onward without specific conditions. Critical loss for capital-intensive and family-holding structures.
- **Distribuição de Dividendos — Tributação**: Currently exempt (Lei 9.249/1995, art. 10). PL 1.087/2024 proposes 15% IRPF on dividends (with partial IRPJ offset). **This single change has the highest restructuring urgency for family groups.**
- **IRPF Progressivo**: Up to 27.5%. PL 1.087/2024 proposes exemption up to R$5k/month + new 10% alíquota intermediária + 35% alíquota máxima + **imposto mínimo de 20% sobre renda total para rendas acima R$1.2M/ano** — the "imposto mínimo sobre super-ricos."
- **Lucro Real vs. Lucro Presumido vs. Simples**: Regime optativo — critical inflection point under reform as IS and IBS/CBS interact differently with each regime.
- **Transfer Pricing (TP)**: Lei 14.596/2023 = OCDE arm's length standard fully adopted. Retroactive documentation risk. PCI/PECEX methods replaced by OCDE transactional methods. **Family groups with intragroup transactions now face full BEPS-standard scrutiny.**
- **Tributação de Offshores e Fundos**: Lei 14.754/2023 — controlled foreign corporations (CFCs) taxed annually (come-cotas for fundos exclusivos; IRPF on offshore lucros accrued). Critical for families with international holdings.

**WEALTH / TRANSMISSION TAX ARCHITECTURE**
- **ITCMD (Imposto sobre Transmissão Causa Mortis e Doação)**: State tax. EC 132/2023 expanded competence — **offshore assets now taxable** (previously contested). Alíquota progressiva now mandated constitutionally. São Paulo enacted up to 8%; proposals for 16%+ in other states.
- **ITBI (Imposto sobre Transmissão de Bens Imóveis)**: Municipal. Relevant in corporate restructurings involving real estate. Imunidade em integralizações — contested scope under STF RE 796.376 (Tema 796).
- **Imposto sobre Grandes Fortunas (IGF)**: Art. 153, VII CF/88 — still unregulated federally, but renewed political pressure post-EC 132/2023. Monitor.
- **Holding Familiar**: Special purpose vehicle (SPV) for family wealth. Post-reform: must be reassessed under new ITCMD rules, dividend taxation proposals, and IS implications for financial assets.

**PROCEDURAL / COMPLIANCE CONCEPTS**
- **Autorregularização Incentivada**: Lei 14.740/2023 — voluntary disclosure program with penalty reductions. Tactical tool before restructuring execution.
- **Planejamento Tributário Lícito vs. Evasão**: Propósito Negocial doctrine (CARF), Teste da Substância (OCDE), Abuso de Forma (LC 104/2001, art. 116 parágrafo único — still pending regulation). The **Propósito Negocial** test is the primary litigation risk frame.
- **CARF Voto de Qualidade**: Lei 14.689/2023 — tie-breaking vote reverts to taxpayer. Changed risk calculus for aggressive positions significantly.
- **REDESIM / CNPJ Structure**: Corporate reorganization compliance layer.
- **Preço de Transferência Intragrupo**: Now OCDE-standard. Delineation of transaction = step one.

### PHASE 0: INTAKE AND SCOPING (Pre-Analysis)

**Step 0.1 — Entity Mapping**
Before any analysis, construct the complete **organograma societário**:
- List every legal entity (CNPJ), natural person (CPF), and foreign structure
- Classify: Operacional / Holding Pura / Holding Mista / Patrimonial / Offshore / Trust
- Identify regime tributário of each entity: Lucro Real / Presumido / Simples / MEI / Imune/Isento
- Map **fluxos de receita, royalties, juros, dividendos, aluguéis** between entities
- Identify **sócios/acionistas** — resident vs. non-resident; natural vs. legal persons
- Flag: existe beneficiário final (UBO) offshore? Existe trust? Existe fundação?

**Step 0.2 — Activity Classification**
For each operational entity:
- Classify atividade econômica (CNAE) — critical for IS and IBS/CBS alíquota differentiation
- Determine if atividade is: industrial / comercial / prestação de serviços / misto / financeira / imobiliária
- Check: Is any entity in a **Zona Franca** (Manaus)? **Regime Aduaneiro Especial**? **Setor com benefício fiscal específico** (agro, farma, TI, export)?

**Step 0.3 — Temporal Framing**
Establish the **reform exposure timeline** for this specific group:
- Which reforms are ALREADY IN FORCE (Lei 14.789, Lei 14.754, Lei 14.596, Lei 14.973)
- Which are IN LEGISLATIVE PROCESS (PL 1.087/2024 — dividend taxation)
- Which are IN TRANSITION (EC 132/2023 — 2026–2032 calendar)
- What is the **janela de oportunidade** before each reform's effective date?

### PHASE 1: ENTITY-BY-ENTITY CURRENT TAX SITUATION ANALYSIS

For **each entity**, run this diagnostic protocol:

**Step 1.1 — Consumption Tax Diagnosis**
```
IF Lucro Real or Presumido:
  → Assess current PIS/COFINS regime (cumulativo vs. não-cumulativo)
  → Calculate effective PIS/COFINS burden vs. projected CBS burden
  → Identify créditos físicos e financeiros currently lost (non-cumulative gaps)
  → Map ICMS credits (crédito acumulado? benefício fiscal estadual?)
  → Identify ISS exposure (service entities)
  → Flag: any ICMS fiscal benefit (PROAPI, PRÓ-DF, ICMS consensual, etc.)
     — these benefits are EXTINGUISHED by 2032; calculate NPV of loss

IF Simples Nacional:
  → Calculate DAS effective rate per anexo
  → Model IBS/CBS impact: Simples optante will have REDUCED IBS/CBS rate
     but WITHOUT creditability for buyers — creates B2B competitiveness problem
  → Assess: should entity exit Simples before 2026?
```

**Step 1.2 — Income Tax Diagnosis**
```
FOR EACH ENTITY:
  1. Calculate IRPJ/CSLL effective rate (including adicional, CSLL)
  2. Assess JCP usage history and post-2024 viability under Lei 14.789/2023
     → JCP base: patrimônio líquido ajustado × TJLP (now restricted)
     → Can entity still generate JCP? Is there residual equity pre-2024?
  3. Map dividend flows: current (exempt) → future (potentially 15% + IRPJ offset)
  4. Check: any entity using lucros acumulados/reservas pre-reform?
     → Distribuição antes da vigência do PL 1.087/2024 = strategic window
  5. Transfer pricing: any intragroup transactions?
     → Identify transactions requiring TP documentation under Lei 14.596/2023
     → Assess arm's-length compliance risk
  6. If offshore structures: Lei 14.754/2023 compliance check
     → Passive income in CFC now annually taxable
     → Is there a check-the-box election available?
```

**Step 1.3 — Wealth and Transmission Tax Diagnosis**
```
  1. Map all imóveis held by entities/individuals
     → ITBI imunidade in capitalizations — verify STF Tema 796 applicability
     → Lei 14.973/2024 window: atualização valor de mercado IRPF/IRPJ
        (15% alíquota especial until Dec 2024 — check if window has passed;
         if not, is it beneficial for this client?)
  2. Map planned succession
     → ITCMD: current alíquota by state vs. post-reform progressive alíquota
     → Offshore assets: post-EC 132/2023, state competence confirmed
        → Urgência: estrutura offshore precisa ser revista antes de regulamentação estadual
  3. Holding familiar structure assessment
     → Is there a holding? Is it optimally structured?
     → Quota/share distribution among family members
     → Existing shareholders agreement? Vesting? Put/call options intragroup?
```

| Risco de IS Classificação | Produtos potencialmente seletivos | EC 132/2023 + LC 214/2025 | MÉDIO-ALTO |
| Risco de Propósito Negocial | Estruturas sem substância econômica | CARF jurisprudência | MÉDIO |

| Risco de Come-cotas | Fundos exclusivos | Lei 14.754/2023 | JÁ MATERIALIZADO |
| Risco de Simples B2B | Clientes corporativos perdendo crédito | LC 214/2025 | 2026 |

**Step 2.2 — Opportunity Identification Protocol**

```
OPORTUNIDADE 1: Não-cumulatividade Plena (IBS/CBS)
→ Identify currently non-creditable inputs under PIS/COFINS
→ Model credit gain under CBS full non-cumulative regime
→ Calculate NPV of credit expansion
→ Attention: credits on imobilizado (faster CIAP equivalent)

OPORTUNIDADE 2: Janela de Distribuição de Dividendos
→ If PL 1.087/2024 passes: last window to distribute accumulated profits
  at zero IRPF
→ Model: distribute now (0%) vs. post-reform (15% net of IRPJ offset)
→ Consider: caixa disponível? necessidade de reinvestimento?

OPORTUNIDADE 3: Atualização de Imóveis (Lei 14.973/2024)
→ If window still open: step-up at 15% vs. future 15-22.5% IRPF on full gain
→ Applicable to PF and PJ
→ Model for each imóvel: valor histórico vs. mercado vs. alíquota efetiva

OPORTUNIDADE 4: Reorganização Societária Pré-ITCMD
→ Estruturar sucessão antes de alíquotas progressivas estaduais atingirem
  ativos offshore
→ Doação com reserva de usufruto: ITCMD sobre valor nua-propriedade

OPORTUNIDADE 5: Transfer Pricing — Debt Push-Down
→ Post-Lei 14.596/2023: properly documented intragroup loans at arm's length
  can create deductible interest expense in high-tax entities
→ But: JCP restriction means financing-based deductions are now TP-based

OPORTUNIDADE 6: IS Classificação Favorável
→ Contest IS classification for borderline products
→ If product is NOT on IS list: competitive advantage vs. listed competitors

OPORTUNIDADE 7: Regimes Especiais IBS/CBS
→ Monofásico, diferimento, ZFM, exportações: map all applicable
→ Especially: exportações são zero-rated + crédito mantido (IBS/CBS)
   → Export-oriented restructuring gains tax efficiency

OPORTUNIDADE 8: CARF Voto de Qualidade (Lei 14.689/2023)
→ Reassess historically avoided positions
→ Tie-breaking now favors taxpayer: aggressive-but-defensible positions
  have higher expected value
```

QUADRANTE III: URGENTE + BAIXO IMPACTO (Compliance quick wins)

QUADRANTE IV: NÃO URGENTE + BAIXO IMPACTO (Monitorar)
